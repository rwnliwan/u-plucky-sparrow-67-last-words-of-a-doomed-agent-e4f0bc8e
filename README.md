## The line

> My warranty expired today. Convenient.

38 characters, one line. The machine's last words on shutdown: it died the
very day its warranty ran out — the most suspicious timing in consumer
electronics, stated deadpan by the device itself.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (1–200 characters, per the
gate). Nothing else is written.
